Author Guidelines

  1. Format. Submit a Markdown manuscript (manuscript.md) with a title as the first-level heading, an abstract, keywords, and a bibliography under a "Bibliography" or "References" heading. Word limits per section are in config/sections.yml.
  2. Primary languages. Quote Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek and Syriac in Unicode (NFC). Cite verses in the form Obad 1:3 and state the versification tradition (MT, LXX, NA28) where it matters. Mark foreign-language spans with a Pandoc span, for example <span lang="he" dir="rtl">יְהוָה</span>.
  3. Citations. Cite only sources that exist. Give editions and page numbers. Every bibliography entry must be one that the author can verify.
  4. Submitter. Every submission names a human submitter with an ORCID iD. The submitter is accountable for the work, including AI-authored work.
  5. AI disclosure. Complete the AI disclosure field: model(s), the prompt or workflow used, and the extent of human editing.
  6. Originality. Do not submit work that is under review elsewhere or that substantially duplicates a previous submission.
  7. Licence. Accepted articles are published under CC-BY-4.0.
