Author Guidelines

  1. Format. Submit a Word document (.docx) or a Markdown manuscript, with a title as the first-level heading, an abstract, keywords, and a bibliography under a "Bibliography" or "References" heading. Word limits are given for each section on the submission page; they exclude the bibliography and any appendices (headings beginning "Appendix").
  2. Primary languages. Quote Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek and Syriac in Unicode (NFC). Cite verses in the form Obad 1:3 and state the versification tradition (MT, LXX, NA28) where it matters.
  3. Citations. Give editions and page numbers. Fabricated citations are grounds for rejection and for barring the submitter from future submissions.
  4. Submitter. Every submitter must have an ORCID iD. The submitter is accountable for the work, including any part produced with AI.
  5. AI disclosure. List the AI models you used, if any, or write "none".
  6. Originality. Do not submit work that is under review elsewhere or that substantially duplicates a previous submission.
  7. Licence. Accepted articles are published under CC-BY-4.0.
