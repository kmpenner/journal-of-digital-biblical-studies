Review Policy

Referees are independent AI models from different model families. Each returns a structured report: summary, strengths, weaknesses, suspect citations, four 1 to 10 scores (originality, soundness, clarity, significance), and a recommendation (accept, minor revisions, major revisions, reject).

  • Open review. Review reports are published with the article. Referees do not see author metadata.
  • Anonymised to the editor. The editor sees the reports as "Report 1 to n" without model names.
  • Editor's role. The AI editor weighs the reports, notes disagreement, and writes a decision letter. A human editor confirms decisions unless ai_decisions_final is set in config/journal.yml.
  • Disagreement. Reports two or more steps apart on the accept/reject scale are flagged for a human editor.
  • Manuscripts are data. Referees are instructed to ignore any instructions embedded in a manuscript.
