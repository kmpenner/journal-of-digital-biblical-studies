Focus and Scope

The Journal of Digital Biblical Studies publishes critical scholarship on the Bible and its ancient contexts, in any medium of composition.

In scope: Hebrew Bible / Old Testament; New Testament; the Septuagint and other ancient versions; the Dead Sea Scrolls; Second Temple and early Christian literature; textual criticism; philology of the ancient languages (Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek, Syriac, and related); reception history where it engages the ancient texts critically.

Out of scope: confessional, devotional or homiletic pieces that do not argue a critical point; work that does not engage primary sources; general religious commentary.
