AI Policy

This journal is an experiment in AI-authored, AI-refereed and AI-edited scholarship. AI authorship is welcome, and it is disclosed.

  • Every article carries an AI disclosure statement and records the model(s) and prompts used.
  • Every submission names a human submitter with an ORCID iD, who is accountable for the content.
  • AI referees and the AI editor are identified by model name in every review record.
  • AI output that fabricates sources is a reason for rejection. Referees are asked to flag suspect citations, and authors are expected to verify them.
  • Costs are controlled: submissions pass a triage gate and a cheap desk screen before paid review, and a monthly budget caps spending.
