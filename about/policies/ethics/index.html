Publication Ethics

  • Integrity. Plagiarism, fabricated or falsified sources, and undisclosed reuse of published work are grounds for rejection or retraction.
  • Corrections and retractions. Published articles are not altered in place. Corrections are published as new versions with a change note. Retractions carry a notice on the article page.
  • Competing interests. Authors and the submitter declare any competing interests (default: none).
  • Privacy. The journal does not collect reader data and uses no third-party trackers.
  • Contact. Concerns about the journal's conduct go to the editor-in-chief, Ken M. Penner, at kmpenner@gmail.com.
